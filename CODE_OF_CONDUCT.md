# Code of Conduct — IEEE YP Industry Hackathon

Our event follows the IEEE Code of Conduct: treat all participants, mentors, judges, and organizers with respect and professionalism.

- Be welcoming and inclusive; no harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior.
- Respect others' work and intellectual property; collaborate fairly within your team.
- Keep submissions and discussions appropriate; no secrets, credentials, or private data in public issues.
- Organizers may remove content or disqualify participants for violations.

Report concerns to Subramanian Narayanan (IEEE SAS YP Chair) — nagusubra@ieee.org.

---

## Code of Conduct & Behavioral Standards

The IEEE Industry Hackathon is committed to offering a safe, inclusive, welcoming, and professional environment for everyone, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, ethnicity, age, or religion.

- **Respectful Conduct:** Treat all participants, mentors, judges, venue staff, and volunteers with professional courtesy and respect. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, stalking, offensive comments, or unwanted physical contact will not be tolerated in-person or on Discord.
- **Academic & Build Integrity:** All submissions must represent original work created during the hackathon window. Any pre-existing open-source code, libraries, or datasets utilized must be clearly disclosed and cited in your GitHub submission.
- **Cyber Security & Fair Play:** Participants must not execute destructive network testing, scraping, denial-of-service attacks, password probing, or unauthorized system access against university networks, sponsor APIs, or fellow participants' devices.
- **Sponsor API Guidelines:** You are encouraged to use sponsor-provided API keys and credits (such as ElevenLabs Creator/Pro coupons) strictly for hackathon prototyping in accordance with published terms. Do not share credentials publicly or exfiltrate private data.
- **Venue Etiquette:** Respect University of Calgary Hunter Hub facilities. Keep aisles clear, maintain food discipline, dispose of waste properly, and assist with standard room resets on Friday and Sunday.

## Team Conflicts & Disclaimer of Organizer Liability

- **No Responsibility for Internal Team Disputes:** Hackathon organizers (IEEE SAS YP), venue hosts, and event sponsors do NOT take responsibility or liability for resolving internal team conflicts, workload allocation disputes, intellectual property/code ownership disagreements, or internal prize-splitting arrangements. Team members are expected to communicate professionally, manage work division collaboratively, and resolve personal or technical differences independently. Prize cheques are issued strictly to the designated Team Captain, and internal distribution of funds remains the sole responsibility of team members.
- **Mandatory Escalation Protocol for Severe Conflicts:** If a team dispute escalates to hostile behavior, personal harassment, Code of Conduct violations, or safety concerns, participants must immediately inform the lead organizer (Subramanian Narayanan at nagusubra@ieee.org or via Discord). Organizers reserve the right, in their sole and absolute discretion, to mediate, reassign team members, disqualify individuals or teams, or revoke event access without refund or recourse if behavioral standards are breached.
