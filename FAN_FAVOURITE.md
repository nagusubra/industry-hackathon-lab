# Fan Favourite - Community Vote via GitHub Issue Reactions

**Prize:** $100 for the **Fan Favourite** team.

The Fan Favourite winner is decided **entirely by community votes** - not by judges. Every project submitted as a **Hackathon Submission issue** can be voted on. The submission issue with the most **👍 (thumbs up)** reactions wins. Voting ends **Sun Oct 4, 4:00 PM MDT**.

---

## How to vote (for everyone)

1. Go to **Issues** in this repo and open a submission issue titled `[Submission] <Team Name> - <Project Title>`.
2. Scroll to the **top post** (the original issue description written by the team - not a comment).
3. In the top-right corner of that post, click the **smiley-face / Add reaction** icon (😄).
4. Click **👍 (thumbs up)**.
5. Done - your vote is counted. Click the 👍 again to remove your vote.

> Only 👍 reactions on the **top post** count. Reactions on comments, other emojis (❤️, 🎉, 😄, etc.), and 👍 on comments do **not** count.

You can vote for as many projects as you like - but only once per project (one 👍 per person per issue).

No GitHub account? You'll need a free GitHub account to react. Sign up at https://github.com/signup, then return to the issue to vote.

---

## How to find the leaderboard

GitHub can sort issues by reactions:

1. Go to **Issues**.
2. In the search bar, filter to submissions, e.g. `is:issue label:submission`.
3. Click **Sort → Most reacted** (URL param `sort:reactions-+1-desc`), e.g.:
   `is:issue label:submission sort:reactions-+1-desc`
4. The issue at the top with the highest 👍 count is currently leading.

Tip: click the 👍 counter on the top post to see the breakdown by emoji - the winner is ranked by **👍 (THUMBS_UP)** only, not total reactions.

---

## Rules

- **What counts:** one 👍 per GitHub user on the issue's **top post**.
- **What doesn't count:** other emojis, reactions on comments, duplicate bot votes.
- **Voting window:** opens when submissions open (Fri Oct 2, 5:00 PM MDT) and closes when winners are announced (Sun Oct 4, 4:00 PM MDT). Organizers will take the final count at judging time.
- **Tie-break:** if two issues tie on 👍, organizers pick the one with the earliest submission time, or announce a tie at their discretion.
- **Fair play:** please don't create fake accounts to inflate votes. Organizers may audit reaction lists (every reaction shows the voter's username) and disqualify manipulated entries.
- Teams may share their own submission issue link and ask friends to vote - that's encouraged!

---

## For submitting teams

- Your project is automatically entered for Fan Favourite when you file your **Hackathon Submission** issue - no extra step needed.
- Share your **issue URL** (not just your repo URL) so voters land directly on the votable post.
- Keep building - votes don't affect judged prizes (1st/2nd/3rd/Best with ElevenLabs).
